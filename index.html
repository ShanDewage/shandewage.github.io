Who I Am as an Engineer

UI/UX Engineer

I’m a UI/UX Engineer and front-end developer who loves turning ideas into engaging, user-friendly digital experiences. My work sits at the intersection of design and code — from crafting clean, intuitive interfaces in Figma and Adobe XD to developing responsive, interactive front-end applications with React, Vue.js, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Throughout my career, I’ve built a variety of projects: POS and Restaurant Management systems, e-commerce websites, mobile apps, and travel platforms. Each project strengthened my ability to merge design thinking with practical implementation, ensuring every product looks great, performs seamlessly, and delivers a smooth user experience.

At Secvision (Pvt) Ltd, I focused on designing interfaces, developing responsive components, and creating marketing visuals, maintaining a consistent and engaging digital presence. My freelance experience taught me to adapt to different project needs, balancing creativity with functional, real-world solutions.

I’m constantly exploring new design trends, experimenting with emerging tools, and refining my front-end skills to craft digital products that are not only visually appealing but also intuitive and impactful.
